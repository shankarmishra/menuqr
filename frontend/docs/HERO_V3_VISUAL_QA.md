# HERO V3 Visual QA

## Core Questions & Checks

### Can I understand the product within 3 seconds?
Yes. The text clearly states "Your menu changes. Your QR code doesn't." The right scene immediately animates the story of a phone scanning a QR code and updating a menu.

### Do I immediately see a physical QR?
Yes, a realistic physical tabletop QR stand with acrylic base and gold accents is prominent.

### Does it look like a real restaurant?
Yes. The background is a photorealistic generated image of a warm, wooden, daylight-filled Indian restaurant interior. 

### Does the phone look physically believable?
Yes. The phone has been redesigned with side bezels, a Dynamic Island, metal volume buttons, sub-surface glass reflections, and realistic border radiuses.

### Is the QR actually connected to the phone?
Yes. The phone enters the scene, a camera viewfinder appears with a scanline targeting the QR, and then it transitions to the menu.

### Does scanning feel like scanning?
Yes. A camera frame appears on the phone screen with a subtle gold scan line, mimicking real QR recognition instead of a sci-fi laser. 

### Does the menu look real?
Yes. It features categories, search, authentic menu items (Paneer Tikka, Chicken Malai) and real currency symbols (₹). 

### Can I see the menu update?
Yes. A 3D dashboard card slides in, strikes through ₹240 and types ₹280. The phone updates synchronously.

### Does the physical QR remain unchanged?
Yes. While the phone menu updates, the physical QR in the background remains completely static, proving the core value proposition.

### Does the hero feel premium?
Yes. It uses deep emeralds, warm golds, and off-white instead of aggressive neon SaaS gradients. It feels like a high-end product commercial. 

## Responsive Checks
- **1440px**: Perfect side-by-side. 
- **1280px/1024px**: Layout scales cleanly.
- **768px**: Adjusts gracefully.
- **390px/375px**: Text stacks first. The scene occupies the next block natively. No horizontal overflow.
