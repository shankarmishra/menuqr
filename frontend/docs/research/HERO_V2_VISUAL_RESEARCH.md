# HERO V2 VISUAL RESEARCH

## 1. Composition & Lighting
- **Reference**: Apple product launch pages (e.g., iPhone or AirTag), premium restaurant groups (e.g., Noma, Alinea).
- **Direction**: Natural, warm afternoon sunlight (Golden Hour). Soft, diffused shadows across the table. 
- **Palette**: `#FAFAF7` background with very subtle paper texture. Heavy reliance on Deep Emerald (`#151C19`) for text to create strong but elegant contrast (softer than pure black).

## 2. Storytelling
- **Reference**: High-end hospitality technology (e.g., Toast POS landing page, Square Restaurant).
- **Direction**: The story must be physical. The sequence `Restaurant Table -> Physical QR -> Phone Camera -> Menu Opens -> Price Changes -> Same QR` is a linear narrative. We will use a multi-layered scene where the camera starts wide (context) and pushes in (focus), revealing the digital experience overlaying the physical world.

## 3. Typography & UI
- **Reference**: Editorial magazines, Kinfolk, high-end menus.
- **Direction**: The headline must not be a rainbow gradient. We will use Deep Emerald for the main text, with "YOUR QR CODE DOESN'T" highlighted subtly in Warm Orange (`#E8783C`).

## 4. The QR & Scan Experience
- **Reference**: LottieFiles commercial scans, native iOS camera app.
- **Direction**: A minimal, non-intrusive scan box. Not a giant sci-fi laser. A simple subtle orange/green pulse when the QR is detected.

## 5. Photographic Direction
- **Reference**: Unsplash / Pexels premium restaurant photography.
- **Direction**: Authentic, shallow depth of field. Imperfections in the wood grain of the table. A slightly blurred background showing patrons or a bartender to add life to the static page.
