# MENUQR TEMPLATE GALLERY V5 — WORLD-CLASS RESTAURANT MENU DESIGN SHOWCASE

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

MenuQR Template Gallery V5 is a complete visual redesign built to match the art direction of a world-class restaurant branding studio. The 15 menu templates feature 100% bespoke visual systems—decoupled from generic SaaS styling—and strictly avoid inline text animations to maintain 100% legibility and physical menu authenticity.

---

## 1. DESIGN INSPIRATION & RESEARCH GROUNDING

Each design system is inspired by authentic restaurant design practices and regional menu craftsmanship:

- **Delhi Luxury Palace / Leather-Bound Style (`Royal Maharaja`)**: Fine double gold line frames, ornate filigree crests, deep wine (`#2A081A`) parchment, right-aligned serif prices, Playfair Display headers.
- **Textile-Inspired Regional Craft (`Rajasthani Heritage`)**: Sandstone tones (`#F7EBE1`), bold terracotta block headers (`#9E3B20`), block-print trim, Cinzel serif typography, regional craft labels.
- **Five-Star Architectural Arch (`Mughal Luxe`)**: Deep emerald (`#0C2920`), Cormorant Garamond font, arched header border frame (`┌────── ARCH ──────┐`), gold foil titles, centered arch categories.
- **Luxury Silk Weave (`Banarasi Gold`)**: Deep burgundy silk (`#28081B`), gold silk weave pattern background, double line gold rules, Philosopher font, gold star headers (`✦ SHIKAR SPECIALS ✦`).
- **Authentic Temple Architecture (`Southern Heritage`)**: Ivory (`#FFFDF7`), maroon (`#6E1B1B`) & brass (`#C28838`) temple geometric top border, Rozha One font, brass horizontal accent rules.
- **High-End Street-Food Brand Identity (`Street Kitchen`)**: Cream & tomato red (`#E85A2A`), bold `Bebas Neue` display font, dish photo cards with MUST TRY & BESTSELLER badges, price tag pills.
- **Fashion Editorial Grid (`Modern India`)**: Asymmetric fashion editorial grid (`Montserrat`), oversized dish titles, thin orange (`#E8783C`) rules, unusual whitespace.
- **Food-First Magazine Composition (`Spice House`)**: Cocoa (`#2B1B17`) & saffron (`#E68A00`) magazine layout, prominent food photograph hero, supporting dish cards, Oswald font.
- **Michelin Tasting Menu Benchmark (`Maison Noir`)**: Nearly black charcoal (`#121212`) & ivory (`#F4EFEA`), `Bodoni Moda` typography, expansive whitespace, hairline gold dividers, zero loud badges.
- **Pristine Minimalist Fine Dining (`Ivory Dining`)**: Warm ivory (`#FFFDF7`), dark serif headings (`Playfair Display`), hairline black dividers, logo mark.
- **Luxury Hotel Level (`Grand Table`)**: Deep hotel emerald (`#0B2B20`), full-width hero header image, gold crest header, two-column menu rows (The Leela / Taj style).
- **Specialty Café & Artisan Bakery (`Coffee House`)**: Espresso brown (`#2E1C14`) & warm cream, handwritten `Caveat` script accents, pastry & coffee photography.
- **Tactile Printed Craft Menu (`Paper Cafe`)**: Natural kraft paper (`#F2EAE1`), hand-drawn sketch borders, handwritten `Amatic SC` category titles, printed grain texture.
- **Mumbai Street Food × Modern Magazine (`Bombay Street`)**: High-energy Mumbai street food, bold `Anton` display typography, bright red/orange price stickers.
- **Universal Modern Minimalism (`Minimal Editorial`)**: Crisp white (`#FAFAF8`), high contrast monochrome typography (`Montserrat`), razor-thin lines, orange accent detail.

## 2. STRICT LEGIBILITY & NO TEXT ANIMATION RULE

- **Static Legibility:** Restaurant names, category titles, dish descriptions, diet indicators, and price tags remain 100% static during scrolling.
- **Showroom Motion Only:** Animations are strictly confined to stage card movement, scale, depth shadow, rotation (-2° to +2°), category filter transitions, metadata fades, and interactive morphs.

---

## 3. SECTION ARCHITECTURE & CONTROLS

- **Category Filter Bar:** `All`, `Indian`, `Luxury`, `Fine Dining`, `Café`, `Street Food`, `Minimal`.
- **Showcase Stage:** Active Card centered (`380px–420px` width, `560px–640px` height) surrounded by partial side cards (`scale: 0.88`, `opacity: 0.45`).
- **Section Height:** Compact ~210vh vertical scroll stage.
- **Interactive Personality Transformer:** Real-time side-by-side comparison (`Royal Maharaja`, `Modern India`, `Minimal Editorial`) with instant 400ms morph.
- **Gallery Inspector Sheet:** High-res phone canvas with architectural specs, typography breakdown, color palette, and `Use This Template` CTA.
