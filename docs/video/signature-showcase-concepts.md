* **Duration:** 8.0 Seconds | **Aspect:** 9:16 Vertical & 16:9 Master
* **Target Segment:** Royal Awadhi palaces, heritage hotel fine-dining, luxury Mughlai banquets.

* **Sound Design:** Deep acoustic Tanpura drone, single crisp Sarod flourish, gentle clinking of royal cutlery.
