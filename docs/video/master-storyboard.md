## 2. Shot-by-Shot Technical Storyboard

### Scene 1: The Sticky Paper Menu Frustration (`00:00 - 00:05`)
* **Visual Action:** Close-up of an upscale or popular Indian dining table. A well-dressed customer picks up a laminated paper menu. The menu corner is dog-eared, greasy from butter chicken gravy, and multiple dish prices are crudely scratched out with a blue ballpoint pen with hand-written "₹280" scribbled beside it. The diner makes a subtle wincing face of distaste.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow macro push-in towards the scribbled pen marks, shallow depth of field (f/1.8).
* **On-Screen Text (Overlay):** `Price change = Pen scribble? ❌` (Red agitation badge).
* **Sound Design (SFX):** Crinkling worn paper sound, slight awkward silence, quiet restaurant murmur.
* **Voiceover (Hinglish):** *"Food 5-star hai, par menu wahi chip-chipa paper? Price badalte hi pen se kaatna padta hai?"*

---

### Scene 2: The 24K Gold Standee & Instant Scan (`00:05 - 00:10`)
* **Visual Action:** Dynamic transition. A sleek, brushed 24K gold-finish MenusQR acrylic/metal standee sits elegantly on a polished wooden table. A customer casually points their smartphone camera at the QR code. Without installing any app, the browser opens instantaneously with a silky, royal restaurant menu.
* **Camera Movement:** Rack focus from the blurred restaurant background onto the sharp gold standee, followed by an over-the-shoulder glide to the phone screen.
* **On-Screen Text (Overlay):** `Zero App Download • Camera Scan • Instant Open` (Gold floating badge).
* **Sound Design (SFX):** Gentle satisfying 'Ding' / camera shutter chime, smooth whoosh transition.
* **Voiceover (Hinglish):** *"Switch karo MenusQR par! Premium 24K gold standee... bina kisi app ke, camera kholo aur instant scan!"*

---

### Scene 3: Half/Full Portions & Direct Table Orders (`00:10 - 00:15`)
* **Visual Action:** Extreme close-up on the iPhone screen displaying the *Rang-e-Punjab* or *Maharaja Table* edition. Customer's thumb taps between **Half (₹220)** and **Full (₹380)** on Paneer Butter Masala. Green Veg indicator glows clearly. Customer taps "Send Order to Kitchen" with table code pre-filled.
* **Camera Movement:** Micro-panning across the phone UI, highlighting tactile button presses and glassmorphic micro-interactions.
* **On-Screen Text (Overlay):** `Half / Full Portions • Pure Veg/Non-Veg • Direct Table Order` (Green check tags).
* **Sound Design (SFX):** Soft tactile haptic tap clicks, subtle confirmation pop.
* **Voiceover (Hinglish):** *"HD photos, Half & Full portion options, aur direct table ordering — sab customer ke phone par!"*

---

### Scene 4: 1-Second Owner Live Updates From Phone (`00:15 - 00:20`)
* **Visual Action:** Split-second cut to the smiling restaurant owner standing near the counter holding their smartphone. With one thumb tap in the MenusQR owner dashboard, they toggle a dish from ₹320 to ₹340 and hit save. Instantly, a diner's phone at Table 4 reflects the new price without reloading the page.
* **Camera Movement:** Medium profile shot of the owner, rapidly whip-panning to a live phone screen on Table 4.
* **On-Screen Text (Overlay):** `1-Second Live Sync • Zero Reprint Cost` (Emerald pulse banner).
* **Sound Design (SFX):** Digital data sync swoosh, positive confirmation chime.
* **Voiceover (Hinglish):** *"Price badalna ho ya item out-of-stock karna ho? Phone se sirf 1 second mein live update!"*

## 3. Safe Zone & Composition Guidelines for 9:16 Vertical Delivery

To ensure zero overlap with native UI overlays across Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts:
* **Top Clearance (0–120px):** Keep clear of Instagram audio tags and header search bars.
* **Bottom Clearance (1600–1920px):** Keep clear of caption descriptions, audio reel titles, and platform CTA overlays.
* **Right Clearance (950–1080px):** Keep clear of Like, Comment, Share, and Remix vertical action buttons.
* **Central Action Zone (Width: 800px, Height: 1200px centered):** Place all hero food shots, phone screens, and pricing text within this high-visibility zone.
