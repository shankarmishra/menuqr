## 3. Broadcast Audio Mix Specifications

* **Voice Track (VO):** Center channel, high-pass filter at 80Hz, gentle compression (3:1 ratio), +2dB presence boost between 2.5kHz–4.5kHz for razor-sharp clarity on smartphone speakers.
* **Music Bed:** Modern electro-acoustic Indian fusion (acoustic guitar strum + subtle dholak heartbeat groove + uplifting synth plucks). Ducks -4.5dB under spoken voice, swells during transitions (`00:04.5`, `00:19.5`, and `00:28.0`).
* **Sound Effects (SFX):** Integrated at -6dB relative to VO to complement without distracting.
