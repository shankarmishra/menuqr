## 3. Phonetic Guide & Delivery Directives for Voice Artist

* **Character Archetype:** The Modern Indian Hospitality Pioneer. Intelligent, empathetic, commanding yet warm. Sounds like a successful restaurant consultant or veteran food entrepreneur who knows the ground reality of running a kitchen in India.
* **Accent & Dialect:** Clean, urban conversational Hinglish (Urban Delhi / Mumbai / Bengaluru hybrid). Natural Hindi grammar combined seamlessly with industry English terminology (`five-star`, `out of stock`, `reprint`, `free trial`).
* **Emotional Arc:**
  * `00:00 - 00:22 (Act I)`: Empathetic agitation. Use lower register, slight conspiratorial sigh, relatable annoyance. Do not sound mocking; sound like you share their frustration.
  * `00:28 - 00:62 (Act II)`: Dynamic lift (+30% vocal brightness). Crisp, crisp enunciation. Upbeat, confident, modern pace.
  * `00:62 - 00:90 (Act III)`: Triumphant, authoritative, celebratory. Strong call-to-action finish with zero doubt.

## 4. Broadcast Sound & Music Mix Specifications

1. **Dialogue / VO Channel:**
   * High-pass filter at 85 Hz to remove room rumble.
   * Subtle multiband compression targeting 200–500 Hz (warmth control) and 3 kHz–6 kHz (presence boost).
   * Level Target: Master integrated loudness for spoken word at **-16 LUFS** (EBU R128 / YouTube & Reels standard).
2. **Music Master Bed:**
   * Modern acoustic-electro fusion bed starting subtly at -18 dB during Act I, rising to -10 dB during transition at `00:28`, and ducking automatically by **-5.5 dB** whenever the VO channel is active.
   * Full swell at Act III finale (`01:24 - 01:30`).
3. **Foley & Sound Effects (SFX):**
   * Paper crinkle, pen scratch, scan ping, haptic phone taps, thermal printer whirr, and cash drawer chime mixed between -12 dB and -8 dB for organic tactile realism.
