# Public Website Visual V2 — Section-by-Section Design Specification

## Section 01 — Cinematic Hero
- **Goal:** Immediately establish physical-to-digital continuity within seconds.
- **Narrative:** Physical QR on table -> activates with subtle glow -> realistic iPhone 16 Pro enters -> camera viewfinder locks on -> laser scan beam sweeps -> phone screen transitions smoothly into the live MENUQR menu.
- **Layout:** 2-Column desktop grid. Left: Bold 64px DM Sans typography + Orange CTAs. Right: Interactive 3D composite featuring physical acrylic QR stand + iPhone 16 Pro mockup.

## Section 02 — One QR, Countless Updates (Live Update Demo)
- **Goal:** Prove that editing the menu in the restaurant dashboard instantly updates the customer's phone without changing the QR code.
- **Layout:** Split-screen side-by-side. Left: Interactive Restaurant Dashboard control panel (Butter Chicken `₹280` -> `₹320`). Right: Photorealistic iPhone showing the live customer menu updating in real-time.
- **Key Badge:** "SAME QR — NO REPRINT REQUIRED" badge prominently anchored.

## Section 03 — Physical QR → Digital Menu
- **Goal:** Visually demonstrate the scanning bridge with perspective depth.
- **Layout:** 3D perspective transform on physical QR stand -> animated glowing beam -> high-resolution iPhone 16 Pro displaying full menu categories.

## Section 04 — Built for the Table
- **Goal:** Show real-world restaurant hardware placement.
- **Layout:** Environmental context cards (Dining Table, Cafe Counter, Reception, Takeaway, Outdoor Terrace) with subtle hover elevation and ambient lighting overlays.

## Section 05 — Template Gallery
- **Goal:** Showcase 10 structurally distinct menu designs.
- **Layout:** Interactive 3D carousel with horizontal drag/swipe and pagination dots. Each template features unique layout architecture, typography, and color palettes.

## Section 06 — Dashboard Demo
- **Goal:** Demonstrate the simplicity of the Restaurant Panel (`/app`).
- **Layout:** High-density browser frame with 4 auto-cycling tabs (Dashboard, Menu Editor, Appearance, QR Center) with clearly marked demonstration metrics.

## Section 07 — Subscription + QR Protection
- **Goal:** Show that QR identity is preserved through subscription expiry and renewal.
- **Layout:** Stepped state machine (Active -> 10-Day Warning -> Expired -> Blocked -> Renewed -> Same QR Active Again) with a constant QR badge alongside the phone screen.

## Section 08 — How MenuQR Works
- **Goal:** Clear 6-step onboarding workflow.
- **Layout:** 6 connected steps with animated step numbers (`01` to `06`), icons, and visual progress connectors.

## Section 09 — Customer Experience
- **Goal:** Highlight customer menu features.
- **Layout:** Centered iPhone 16 Pro mockup demonstrating search bar, Veg/Non-Veg filter pills (`#34b56a` / `#ff5e4d`), and scrollable dish cards.

## Section 10 — Pricing
- **Goal:** Transparent annual subscription plan.
- **Layout:** Featured card with gold accent borders, clear feature list, and primary CTA.

## Section 11 — FAQ
- **Goal:** Resolve objections directly.
- **Layout:** Accordion with 8 key questions covering QR permanence, price edits, subscription renewal, and app-less scanning.

## Section 12 — Final CTA & Footer
- **Goal:** Cinematic closing banner and multi-column brand footer.
- **Layout:** Centered headline *"Print it once. Update it anytime."* with primary orange action button and brand links.
