# PUBLIC WEBSITE UI RESEARCH

## 1. Premium SaaS Landing Pages (Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Notion)
- **Source URL**: Linear (linear.app), Vercel (vercel.com), Stripe (stripe.com), Notion (notion.so)
- **Observed Pattern**: "Product is the Demo" approach. High-density, interactive, in-page product UI rather than abstract illustrations. Techno-Futurist (dark mode, bento grids, WebGL) vs Editorial (cream backgrounds, typography focus).
- **Why it works**: Shows capability immediately, builds trust through transparent functionality, feels high-performance.
- **MENUQR Reinterpretation**: Since we are offering a digital menu management product, our homepage must be a high-fidelity interactive demo of the menu creation and updating process. We will use the brand palette (Dark Emerald, Card Surface) to create a premium, slightly techno-futurist yet accessible feel.
- **License/Copyright consideration**: Will not use their actual assets, but will adopt the principle of embedded product demos using our own UI components.

## 2. Restaurant Technology Websites (Toast, Square, TouchBistro)
- **Source URL**: Toast (pos.toasttab.com), Square for Restaurants (squareup.com), TouchBistro (touchbistro.com)
- **Observed Pattern**: Mobile-first design, connected sync between POS and online interfaces, clear call-to-actions for ordering and booking.
- **Why it works**: Addresses the operational efficiency restaurateurs care about.
- **MENUQR Reinterpretation**: Emphasize how MENUQR solves the friction of changing menus. Highlighting the split-screen view of "edit here, update there" to show seamless syncing. Focus on mobile view of the final menu product.
- **License/Copyright consideration**: General industry best practices; no direct asset copying.

## 3. QR Code Animation & Interactive Product Storytelling
- **Source URL**: Various Dribbble/Awwwards sites focusing on hardware-to-software bridges.
- **Observed Pattern**: Tactile, physical scanning animations (camera viewfinder locking onto a QR code, 3D tilt effects, physical sticker printing animations).
- **Why it works**: Bridges the gap between the physical restaurant table and the digital interface on the phone.
- **MENUQR Reinterpretation**: A scroll-driven animation where a physical QR sticker on a table is scanned by a phone, smoothly transitioning the screen from the camera view to the live digital menu.
- **License/Copyright consideration**: Ensure any SVG or animations are custom-built or use open-source MIT licensed libraries (like Framer Motion) for implementation.

## 4. Scroll-Driven Animation Patterns
- **Source URL**: Apple product pages, web.dev scroll-driven animations.
- **Observed Pattern**: Sections that stick while content changes (sticky positioning), elements scaling or fading in based on scroll depth.
- **Why it works**: Keeps the user engaged, controls the pace of information delivery.
- **MENUQR Reinterpretation**: Use for the "One QR, Countless Updates" section and the "Subscription Protection" timeline to walk the user through the narrative without requiring them to click.
- **License/Copyright consideration**: Standard CSS/JS techniques.

## Summary
The website will rely heavily on **product-led storytelling**. The aesthetic will be premium (dark mode using Dark Emerald and Card Surface), utilizing precise, fast animations to demonstrate the core value proposition: physical QR codes leading to instantly updatable digital experiences.
