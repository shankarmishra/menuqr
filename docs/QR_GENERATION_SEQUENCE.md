# CINEMATIC QR PRODUCTION ANIMATION SEQUENCE

## 1. Sequence Overview
`src/components/qr-studio/QRGenerationSequence.tsx` implements a 7-phase physical production animation sequence that transforms QR creation from a standard utility into a memorable commercial product experience.

## 2. Production Phases
1. **Preparing:** Registration lines and crop marks appear in a Mac-styled studio modal (800ms).
2. **Material Selection:** Blank physical card/sticker sheet slides into frame with realistic drop shadows (600ms).
3. **Brand Printing:** MENUQR wordmark and restaurant business name print onto the card surface (700ms).
4. **QR Generation:** High-resolution SVG QR modules progressively resolve while a laser scan line moves across the QR frame (1200ms).
5. **Details Application:** "Scan for Menu" callouts and table number badges resolve (500ms).
6. **Finishing Touches:** Card surfaces settle with realistic perspective lighting (400ms).
7. **Ready State:** "Your permanent QR is ready" green badge reveals high-res PNG/SVG download actions.
